Privacy Policy for MannS Parlor

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Application ID: com.maans.parlormanagement

Welcome to MannS Parlor (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our salon owners, staff, and clients. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, collect, use, and protect your information when you use our salon management mobile application (the “App”).

Core Commitment: MannS Parlor is an internal salon & beauty parlor management tool. We respect your data confidentiality and do not sell, rent, or trade any personal or business data to third-party advertising companies.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide smooth salon management, counter billing, and appointment scheduling services, the App collects the following details:

  • Customer & Client Information: Client name and mobile contact number provided when booking appointments, selecting services, or registering walk-in counter orders.
  • Staff Information: Stylist and salon staff names, mobile numbers, specialized skills (e.g. Hair stylist, Facial expert), and order assignment statuses.
  • Salon Business Details: Parlor name, registered business email address, salon phone number, city, and address configured by the system administrator.
  • Order & Billing Records: Appointment timestamps, chosen services, assigned products, bill amounts, and payment mode status (e.g., Cash, UPI, Card). (Note: We do not process or store sensitive banking details like credit card numbers or PINs directly in the App.)
  • App Preferences: Language preferences (English, Hindi, Gujarati) and theme mode stored locally on your device for user experience.

2. How We Use the Information

The collected information is solely utilized for core application functionality, including:

  • Managing day-to-day salon operations, advance bookings, walk-ins, bridal appointments, and home service requests.
  • Allocating staff members and stylists to specific customer appointments.
  • Tracking inventory stock and retail salon products.
  • Generating real-time daily revenue, active appointments, and completed order dashboards.
  • Authenticating authorized salon accounts and system administrators.

3. Cloud Storage & Third-Party Service Providers

To provide real-time cloud synchronization, data persistence, and security, our App utilizes trusted infrastructure services:

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore: Used for real-time cloud database storage and authentication synchronization. All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). You can review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain your salon management and appointment data only as long as necessary to provide business services.

  • User Account Creation: User accounts for salons are set up and managed by the Super Administrator. There is no public user registration inside the App.
  • Data Deletion Requests: If you are a salon owner or a customer who wants your contact details, appointments, or salon account deleted permanently from our database, you can initiate a deletion request at any time by contacting our support team at mansiflutter79@gmail.com. Upon verification, your data will be deleted within 30 days.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement robust administrative and technical security measures to ensure your data is safeguarded against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. All data transmissions between the application and Google Cloud Firestore servers are protected with end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our App is designed for salon businesses, professional stylists, and adults (18 years of age and older). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational procedures or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
